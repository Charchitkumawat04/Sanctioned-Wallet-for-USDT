## 🪙 USDT (Tether) - Overview & History

[View Dashboard on Dune](https://dune.com/charchitku/sanctioned-wallet-for-usdt)

**Launch Year:** 2014  
**Issuer:** Tether Ltd. (originally Realcoin)  
**Type:** Centralized stablecoin  
**Peg:** 1 USDT = ~1 USD  
**Blockchains:** Ethereum (ERC-20), Tron (TRC-20), Solana, BNB Chain, Algorand, Avalanche, Polygon, Omni, and others.

### 🔑 Key Milestones

- **2014:** USDT launched as Realcoin, later renamed to Tether.
- **2015:** Starts trading on Bitfinex.
- **2017-2018:** Rapid rise in usage during the crypto boom.
- **2019:** Begins operating across multiple blockchains (Ethereum, Tron).
- **2021:** Crosses $60B in circulation, becomes dominant stablecoin by volume.
- **2022:** Faced scrutiny over reserve transparency; released assurance reports.
- **2023:** Introduced stronger KYC/AML compliance; froze wallets tied to illicit activity.
- **2024:** Enhanced reporting of reserves; moved some backing to U.S. T-bills and gold.
- **2025:** Continues to be the most used stablecoin in global crypto trading.

### 🚫 Banned/Restricted Addresses

Tether has the ability to freeze USDT in wallets associated with:
- Sanctions lists (e.g., OFAC)
- Fraud/scam operations
- Hacks or stolen funds
- Law enforcement requests

Over 1,000+ wallet addresses have been frozen since 2020.

### 🔍 Compliance Tools

- On-chain freezing functions available to Tether
- Regular reporting of frozen addresses
- Coordination with regulators and exchanges
